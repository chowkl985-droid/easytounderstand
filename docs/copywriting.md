## 1. Gumroad 銷售頁（長文）

### English（主推）

You know that feeling? You're staring at an English academic paper, a policy document, or a legal contract — and every word makes sense individually, but together they're just... noise.

**MakeItEasy** was built for exactly this.

Paste any text or upload a PDF → it auto-splits into paragraphs → pick your difficulty → AI rewrites it in plain, simple language — in the language **you're most comfortable with** (Cantonese, Mandarin, or English).

**Three difficulty levels:**
- 🎒 **Kid** — Stories and simple analogies, like a teacher explaining to a child
- 🎓 **Teen** — Casual, everyday language, like chatting with a friend
- 💼 **Working Adult** — Straight to the point, focused on key takeaways

**Cross-language superpower:** English paper → explained in Cantonese. Chinese policy document → explained in plain English. Read in any language, understand in yours.

**7 AI engines supported:** DeepSeek / GPT-4o / Claude / Gemini / Grok / Qwen / Custom API — bring your own key, use whatever works for you.

| Plan | What you get |
|------|-------------|
| **Free** | 10 text + 3 PDF uses per day (bring your own API key) |
| **Pro — $39 USD** | One-time purchase. Unlimited text & PDF forever. |

**$39 once vs ChatGPT at $20/month — pays for itself in 2 months, then free forever.**

No subscription. No recurring fees. Buy once, use for life.
